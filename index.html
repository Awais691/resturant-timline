A little about us

A dining room with its sleeves rolled up.

Saffron & Sage began with a simple idea: thoughtful food should feel generous, never formal. Our kitchen brings local produce, old family flavours, and a curious eye to the same table.

Come for a quick supper. Stay for one more cup of chai.
