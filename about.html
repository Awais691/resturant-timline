Our story

A good meal is never just about the food.

It's the hands that grew it, the people who made it, and the ones around the table.

How it started

A kitchen that feels like coming home.

Saffron & Sage is a fictional restaurant concept built around a feeling many of us know: a table that gets louder as the evening goes on, another dish arriving before anyone asks, and time moving a little more slowly.

Our imagined Lahore dining room takes cues from the places where people cook with what they have and make room for one more. The menu follows that same instinct: local when we can, thoughtful always.

Our way of cooking

Start with what's good.

We look for ingredients at their best, then give them the space to be themselves. A bright herb chutney can bring a whole plate into focus; a slow pot can make a rainy evening feel right.

There are family recipes in the mix, but nothing is fixed. The menu changes when the market gives us a reason to change it.

Our kitchen

Meet Chef Amina Qureshi.

In this restaurant concept, Chef Amina brings together the food she grew up with and the ingredients she discovers each morning. Her kitchen is curious, practical, and happiest when a plate makes someone pause before the first bite.

Her north star: care for the ingredient, respect the craft, and never let the room feel stiff.

What matters here

Good food, with good sense.

01

Buy thoughtfully

We imagine working with growers and makers close to home, choosing what's in season, and wasting as little as possible.

02

Cook with care

Every part of a dish has a reason to be there. We keep the technique in service of flavour, not the other way around.

03

Make room

Hospitality should be generous and easy. Come dressed up or straight from the day; we'll make you comfortable.

The room

Soft light. Open doors. One more chair.

Our imagined space is a mix of old wood, handmade details, and the gentle hum of a full dining room. There's no dress code beyond arriving hungry.
